VillainThe Stars

Moon Veil Aetherion

Blade Duelist · Bladework

“I don't fight for peace. I fight because they mess up my hairstyle.”

Story

A shadow that moves through the Dark Criminal Forest's underworld, as feared for his blade as he is known for his vanity. He once served in the Blade Military before leaving to join the Stars.

Powers

  • A duelist whose twin blades move like extensions of moonlight and shadow.

Personality

Vain, sharp-tongued, and dangerously composed in a fight.
